# Chrono

Adds Chrono to the game, a survivor conceptualised and commissioned by Rypling! Chrono is a crit-focused juggernaut that passively ramps up their power, becoming capable of controlling most battlefields with ease. Upon encountering a particularly formidable foe, Chrono can spend all of their gained power to unleash their nigh-unsurvivable skill ***Time Collapse***.

## Passive

Crit chance over 100% is converted into crit damage. Upon attacking an enemy with their primary, Chrono also gains ***Temporal Rift***. As you increase your ***Temporal Rift*** level towards 100%, Chrono gains up to 35% extra crit chance, 70% crit damage, and 40% movement speed.

## Primary

There are two simple primary attacks to choose from. Chrono can slash with their spear with ***Eon's Edge***, or instead hurl it with ***Split-Second Throw***. While ***Eon's Edge*** boasts more raw damage and a higher attack rate, ***Split-Second Throw*** has the advantage of distance, which can enable Chrono to more safely build ***Temporal Rift***.

## Secondary

The secondary skills are a trade-off between control and damage. ***Time Piercer*** offers a way to reliably slow, stun, or even freeze enemies, according to your ***Temporal Rift*** level. It has fairly low damage and accuracy, but can be used as a burst option in a pinch. On the contrary, ***Event Horizon*** is a sluggish but high-damage option. While it also slows enemies, and can freeze on a critical hit, it doesn't offer the same degree of battlefield control as ***Time Piercer***.

## Utility

The choice of Chrono's utility is largely dependent on whether or not you are battling with allies. Fighting alone, ***Echo of Tomorrow*** is more optimal for Chrono. It's effective in all situations, as in addition to being a versatile movement tool, it can freeze enemies, provides Chrono healing proportional to their ***Temporal Rift***, and finally grants a temporary buff to damage and move speed. But with allies, it may be preferable to run ***Continuum Freeze*** instead. This alternate skill unleashes a ward which freezes nearby enemies and grants all allies within the ward lifesteal, plus a bonus to crit chance and crit damage. The lifetime of the ward is proportional to Chrono's ***Temporal Rift*** level.

## Special

Chrono only has one special skill, ***Time Collapse***. With this skill, Chrono can trade all of their ***Temporal Rift*** to summon a focused and overwhelmingly powerful blast that always crits and has damage proportional to how much ***Temporal Rift*** was spent. By itself, the skill only deals a measly 100% base damage. The true power of this skill lies in the fact that its damage is a multiplication of **all the damage you have dealt in the last 10 seconds**. Used effectively, ***Time Collapse*** can allow Chrono to utterly wipe out any target. This skill has a minute cooldown, and will reset Chrono back to square one, so it is best to hold onto it for the right moment.
